Workshop Series

SBD Foundations: Squat, Bench & Deadlift

A welcoming, low-pressure introduction to the three lifts that matter — zero experience required. Thursdays, 6:30–7:30 PM: August 27, September 3, and September 10 at HVPL in Highland, NY.

Register Now See the Sessions

Build confidence with the barbell in a welcoming, low-pressure environment. Whether you have never touched a barbell before or want to refine your mechanics, this three-part workshop series introduces the foundational powerlifting movements through clear, structured instruction. HVPL membership not required.

How It Works

Three simple ground rules that keep this approachable, whichever session you come to.

One Lift, One Hour, Once a Week

Focused, digestible sessions designed to help you feel comfortable under the bar without overwhelming you.

Flexible Attendance

Each session stands alone. Drop in for the specific lift you want to practice, or take all three to get a solid grasp of the basics.

Small Group Focus (5–10 Lifters)

Small class sizes ensure you get plenty of time with the bar and personalized, real-time feedback.

How Each Hour Runs

Small groups, rotating through the racks, so nobody stands around waiting.

1

Overview & warm-up

What we're covering, what to expect, and a warm-up that gets you ready to lift.

2

Safety and the equipment

The equipment for that night's lift — combo racks and safeties for the squat and bench, jacks and plate loading on the platform for the deadlift — plus how to load a bar correctly and safely so the setup works for you instead of against you.

3

Technique instruction

The lift broken down into the pieces that matter, with demonstrations and the cues we actually use when coaching.

4

Hands-on lifting in small groups

We split into groups so you work together and keep moving — one lifting, others loading and spotting. Both coaches circulate the whole time, watching for safety and giving individual feedback.

5

Common mistakes and Q&A

The errors we saw on the floor, how to fix them, and open questions before you head out.

Good to Know

Who it's for

Beginner through advanced-beginner lifters. Perfect if you've never touched a barbell, or if you've been lifting a while and want to refine your mechanics with real eyes on your technique. Come on your own or bring a friend — a more advanced series is planned for later.

Group size

Capped at 10 lifters per session with a minimum of 5 to run. We keep it small on purpose so it stays hands-on and everyone gets individual attention.

Registration deadline

Registration for each session closes 48 hours before it starts, or as soon as it fills — whichever comes first. Paid spots come first, so don't wait.

Minimum age

16 and up. Anyone under 18 needs a parent or guardian to sign the waiver during registration. Parent and kid signing up together is very welcome.

Refunds

Signed up for a single session? Full refund with 48 hours' notice before that session. Signed up for all three? Full refund with 48 hours' notice before the first session.

What to bring

Comfortable workout clothes you can move in, flat shoes or lifters if you have them, water, and a willingness to learn. No belt, sleeves, or wraps needed. You don't need to be an HVPL member to attend.

Zero Experience Required

No prior strength training experience is needed — wear comfortable workout clothes and bring a willingness to learn. You will walk away with a strong starting foundation in each lift, a better understanding of proper technique, and a clear path toward continuing your strength journey.
